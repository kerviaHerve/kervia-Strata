# Code of conduct

We want technical discussion to be useful, respectful and open to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss the work and its evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment and discrimination.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's credentials, contact details or private conversations.
- Give credit, accept corrections and describe disagreements with concrete technical reasons.
- Keep reports and reviews relevant to the project. Spam and threats are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent. GitHub's private **Report abuse** option is available for conduct violations; do not create a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
